 Hexpionage Rules Clarifiers

Movement of Intel:

* When two or more pieces of intel move into the same space, they stack on top of each other. If for any reason multiple intel are on the same space, they all share it. Any agent that moves onto that space gains all intel there. All stacked intel trickles like normal, and different intel colors can trickle in different downard directions.  
* When intel is on the bottom row, it trickles off the board and is returned to the supply.  
* When trickling intel, move all pieces simultaneously. Then any agents with honeypots are removed from the game, and the intel possessed by the. is returned to the supply. Then all intel belonging to agents with over three intel return their intel to the supply.  
* It might be helpful to trickle intel row by row starting from the bottom to help keep track of which intel has already been moved.

Agents

* To spend intel/actions you have to be able to resolve the action.  
* The Comms Specialist can move intel up & left or up & right or down & left or down & right. The move is not restricted by the direction the intel trickled downwards from.  
* When an agent is pinned, its abilities can still be used.  
* Smugglers cannot swap pinned agents.  
* Engineers may place blockades on spaces that have intel. Blockades that are placed on spaces with intel prevent that intel from trickling down and other intel may not move into a blockaded space.  
* A Comms Specialist cannot move intel into a blockaded space.  
* If two adjacent spaces are blockaded, then intel directly above and between the two blockaded spaces does not trickle down. The same applies if a Comms Specialist attempts to move intel vertically, and both adjacent spaces in the direction of movement are blockaded.
